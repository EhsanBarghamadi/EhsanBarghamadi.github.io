How I Think, Learn & Build

My journey in backend development has been shaped largely by curiosity about how systems work. When implementing a feature, I don't stop at making it work. I want to understand why a particular solution was chosen, how data flows through the system, and how technical decisions affect other parts of the application.

Through independent development, I've worked on two projects: Digichera and Event Planet. These projects have given me the opportunity to put what I've learned into practice, tackle different backend challenges, and grow my experience in designing and building software systems.

I believe good code is more than code that simply works. It should also be readable, maintainable, and easy to extend. That's why I care about code structure, testing, and considering different scenarios. I try to learn something new at every stage of development.
